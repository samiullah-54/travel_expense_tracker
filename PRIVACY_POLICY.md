# Privacy Policy for Travel Expense Tracker

**Effective Date:** March 18, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to the **Travel Expense Tracker**. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your data. Because this application was designed specifically with your privacy and convenience in mind as a personal portfolio and utility tool, **our data practices are extremely straightforward: we do not collect, transmit, or share your data.**

## 2. Data We Collect (None)
The Travel Expense Tracker **does not** collect, transmit, distribute, or sell any of your personal information to external servers, third-party trackers, or cloud services. 

## 3. Local Storage & How Your Data is Handled
All data entered into the Travel Expense Tracker is strictly kept securely encapsulated within your mobile device's local sandbox environment.

* **Profile Data:** The name you provide on the Welcome Screen is stored locally on your device using standard shared preferences to personalize your experience.
* **Database Data:** Your tours, itineraries (city stays), and financial expenses (amounts, notes, categories) are stored in an encrypted local **SQLite Database** (`sqflite`) directly on your phone's hard drive.
* **Media & Attachments:** When you use your camera or photo gallery to attach receipts to an expense, the app creates a local copy of that image within the app's isolated document directory. 

None of the data mentioned above ever leaves your device. 

## 4. Internet Connectivity
The application is entirely offline and does not require an active internet connection to function. We do not use backend APIs, analytics, crashlytics, or remote databases.

## 5. Third-Party Sharing
Because your data is never transmitted out of your device, it is impossible for us to share your data with any third parties. 

* **App Sharing:** If you manually share the `.apk` installation file of this app with a friend (e.g., via WhatsApp), you are only sharing the application software. Your local SQLite database and media sandbox are tied strictly to your phone's operating system environment and **will not** be transferred to their device.

## 6. Your Rights & Data Deletion
You maintain complete ownership of your data at all times.
* You can delete individual tours, cities, or expenses within the app using the "Swipe-to-Delete" feature.
* You can completely wipe all data entirely by uninstalling the application from your mobile device or clearing the app's storage in your phone's Settings menu.
