# Travel Expense Tracker - User Manual

Welcome to the **Travel Expense Tracker**, a personalized application meticulously crafted for seamless business travel management across Pakistan.

## 1. Getting Started
When you open the app for the very first time, you will be greeted by a Welcome Screen.
1. Enter your **Name** in the provided text field.
2. Tap **Continue**. 
3. *Note: This name is saved locally on your device to provide a personalized experience every time you open the app.*

## 2. Managing Your Business Tours
The **Dashboard** (Home Screen) is your command center. It displays all your scheduled business tours.

### Adding a New Tour
1. Tap the **+** (Plus) Floating Action Button at the bottom right.
2. Enter the **Tour Title** (e.g., "Lahore to Karachi Business Trip").
3. Set your **Start Date** and **End Date**.
4. Add any optional **Notes/Description**.
5. Tap **Save Tour**.

### Editing or Deleting a Tour
* **View/Edit:** Tap on any Tour card to enter its details.
* **Delete:** **Swipe Left** on the Tour card. A confirmation dialog will appear. *Warning: Deleting a Tour will permanently delete all cities and expenses associated with that tour.*

## 3. Managing City Stays
Once inside a Tour, you can map out your itinerary by logging the cities you visit.

### Adding a City Stay
1. Tap the **+** (Location) button at the bottom right.
2. Enter the **City Name** (e.g., "Multan").
3. Set your **Arrival Date** and **Departure Date**.
4. Tap **Save**.

### Editing or Deleting a City Stay
* **View/Edit:** Tap on any City card to enter its expense list.
* **Delete:** **Swipe Left** on the City card to remove it from your tour. *Warning: This action will delete all expenses logged under this specific city.*

## 4. Keeping Track of Expenses
Inside a specific City Stay, you can log all your financial transactions in **PKR**. Your total expenses for that city will calculate automatically at the top of the screen.

### Logging an Expense
1. Tap the **+** (Cart) button at the bottom right.
2. Enter the **Amount** in PKR.
3. Select a **Category** from the dropdown menu (`Fuel`, `Tolls`, `Hotel`, `Food`, `Ironing`, `Other`).
   * *If you select **Other**, a new text field will appear allowing you to type specifically what the expense was (e.g., "Client Gifts").*
4. Add optional **Notes/Description**.
5. Verify the **Date** (defaults to today).
6. **Attach an Image/Receipt:** Tap the attachment button to use your Camera or select an image from your Photo Gallery.
7. Tap **Save Expense**.

### Editing or Deleting an Expense
* **Edit:** Tap on the expense card to update the amount, category, or replace the receipt photo.
* **Delete:** **Swipe Left** on the expense card to permanently remove it and adjust your total.
